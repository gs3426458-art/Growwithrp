What is WordPress website development?

WordPress is flexible, easy to update and works with thousands of plugins. A good WordPress site is lightweight, secure and structured so Google can crawl it, not a heavy theme loaded with unused plugins.

What we do

  • Custom or Elementor-based responsive design
  • Speed work: image compression, caching and clean code
  • SEO setup: titles, schema, XML sitemap and clean URLs
  • Contact forms, WhatsApp button and Google Analytics
  • Security basics: updates, backups and login protection
  • Training so your team can edit content themselves

Frequently asked questions

How much does a WordPress website cost in India?+

It depends on pages, design and features such as booking or payments. We quote after understanding your needs, so you pay only for what you need.

How long does it take to build a WordPress website?+

Small business sites usually take a few weeks, depending on content and approvals.

Is WordPress good for SEO?+

Yes, when built cleanly. Fast hosting, a lightweight theme, proper headings and schema matter more than the platform.

Can you redesign my site without losing rankings?+

Yes. We keep URLs and content that perform, set up redirects and check Search Console after launch.

Should I choose WordPress or Shopify?+

WordPress suits service and content websites. Shopify suits online stores.
