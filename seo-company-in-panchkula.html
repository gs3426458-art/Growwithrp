AEO: Answer Engine Optimisation

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization which concentrates on providing clear and useful answers.

This translates to a web page which will: Contain answers to user questions.

Clearly stated. Use descriptive headings and subheadings. Keep descriptions brief and relevant. Include related FAQs.

Write clear language.

Showcase expertise. For example, a page on an SEO company will go into detail on “What Does An SEO company Do” rather than explaining what it does as an afterthought.

GEO: Generative Engine Optimisation

GEO, as it is sometimes described, is how you optimise your information in a way so that it is of more value to be generated in a generative search environment.

If your business is a local business, GEO means: Clearly list your business name Clearly list what services you offer Tell people where you are Located Specify which areas you cover Outline your expertise and experience Let people know how to contact you Include other useful information, such as your history, reviews and your USP’s This is not about trying to manipulate AI to get results it is about providing truly excellent content.

AIO: AI Optimisation

There is a risk AIO can be an excuse to churn out tons of generic articles. Google’s newest guidelines specifically mention “high-quality, unique content”, and “existing best practices for SEO remain foundational for being visible in generative AI features“.
