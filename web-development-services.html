WordPress Website Development in India

WordPress suits service businesses, hospitals, clinics, schools, real estate firms and any site that needs regular content updates. We connect Meta Pixel and Google Analytics, so your Meta Ads can track purchases. We can also migrate products from WooCommerce or another platform with redirects that protect your SEO.

WordPress vs Shopify: Which Is Better for Your Business?

  • Choose WordPress for service websites, hospital and clinic sites, real estate, B2B and content-heavy blogs.
  • Choose Shopify for online stores that need a catalogue, secure checkout, shipping tools and quick set-up.
  • Choose WooCommerce (on WordPress) when you want full control and already run a content site.
  • Choose custom code only for special features that platforms cannot handle.

Decide by goal and budget, not by trend. We recommend the platform after a short consultation.

Mobile-First and Fast Websites for Indian Users

Google mainly uses the mobile version of a page for indexing, as described in its guide to mobile-first indexing. It also measures real user experience through Core Web Vitals: loading speed, interactivity and visual stability. We compress images, remove unused code, use reliable hosting and keep layouts stable, so pages feel quick even on slower networks.

SEO-Friendly Website Development

SEO works best when the structure is right from day one. We include clean URLs, one H1 per page, clear titles and descriptions, internal links, an XML sitemap, image alt text and schema markup. Then our SEO and AI SEO services can build on a solid base, instead of fixing problems later.

What Affects Website Development Cost in India?

Cost depends on the number of pages, design depth, platform, features such as booking, payments or multi-language support, content needs and ongoing support. Content Checklist Before We Start

Prepare a description of each service or product, real photos, customer reviews you can show, prices or price ranges where possible, and your address, hours and map link. Choose one person on your side to approve content quickly, because content delays are the most common reason a website project slips.
