Case Study: Real Search and Social Results

Two real examples from Grow with RP. We show the numbers as they are, explain what they mean, and say what we would do next. Results vary by business, and we never promise a specific outcome.

Get a free consultationSee our services

Example 1: Google Search Console, four weeks

This is the performance report from our own website for Web search, from 5 October to 2 November 2026.

805Total clicks
59.6KTotal impressions
7.8Average position
1.4%Average CTR

What the numbers mean

  • Impressions show how often our pages appeared in Google results. Daily impressions grew from roughly 1.4K to about 2.8K during the period.
  • Average position of 7.8 means that, on average, our pages appeared near the top of the first page for the searches that showed them.
  • CTR of 1.4% means fewer people clicked than saw the page. This is the clearest opportunity: visibility is there, clicks are not yet as high as they could be.

What we would do next

  • Rewrite titles and meta descriptions for the pages with the most impressions and lowest CTR.
  • Add short, direct answers and FAQs to pages that match real questions.
  • Improve internal links between related service and location pages.
  • Check speed and mobile experience on the pages that get the most visits.

Example 2: Organic Instagram content, one gift shop

A Kaithal-based gift shop worked with us on planned, consistent organic Reels and posts. In 30 days the content earned more than 114,000 organic Instagram views, with zero paid ad spend.

What made the difference was simple: a clear posting plan, short videos with a strong first two seconds, captions that matched what customers asked about, and steady posting rather than one-off bursts. We cannot promise the same reach for every account. Views depend on the niche, the content and the audience.

What these case studies do not show

  • They are not a promise of rankings, leads or sales for your business.
  • They do not show revenue, because we do not have that data for these examples.
  • Our own website is a single example. It is not a benchmark for every industry.
