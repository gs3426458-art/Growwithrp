Meta Ads Agency in India: Facebook and Instagram Ads Management for Leads and Sales

Get a Free ConsultationAll Services

Grow with RP is a Meta ads agency in India that runs Facebook and Instagram ads for businesses that want enquiries, calls, WhatsApp chats and sales. Meta Ads reach people by city, age, interest and behaviour, which suits Indian businesses with local customers. Done well, they bring qualified leads within days. Done badly, they burn budget on clicks that never convert. As a Digital Marketing Company in India, we manage campaigns from set-up to reporting, and our management fee is always separate from your ad budget.

Meta Ads Management Services We Offer

  • Facebook ads management: campaigns for page growth, traffic, leads and sales.
  • Instagram ads management: Reels, stories and feed ads for visual brands.
  • Meta lead generation ads: instant forms that collect names and phone numbers.
  • WhatsApp click-to-message ads: ads that open a chat with your team.
  • Retargeting campaigns: ads for website visitors and past engagers.
  • Ecommerce and catalogue ads: product-focused campaigns for online stores.
  • Local awareness ads: reach people near your shop, clinic or office.

What Our Meta Ads Management Includes

  • Goal and offer review: what you sell, who buys and what a lead is worth.
  • Account and tracking set-up: Business Manager, Meta Pixel and Conversions API where technically supported.
  • Campaign structure: campaigns, ad sets and ads organised for testing.
  • Audience targeting: location radius, age, interests, custom audiences and lookalikes where data allows.
  • Creative production: images, carousels and short videos, including AI-assisted video reviewed by people.
  • Landing pages and forms: a fast page or in-app form that makes enquiring easy.
  • Weekly optimisation: budget shifts, creative swaps and audience refinements.
  • Monthly reporting: spend, cost per lead and what we will test next.

Meta Lead Generation Ads for Indian Businesses

Lead generation ads suit clinics, coaching centres, real estate, education and service businesses. People tap the ad, fill a short form and your team calls them. We keep forms short, ask only what you need and set up quick follow-up, because speed matters: a lead that waits a day often goes to a competitor.

WhatsApp Ads for Businesses in India

WhatsApp is the main way many Indian customers talk to businesses. Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat directly from Facebook or Instagram. They work well for retail, real estate, healthcare enquiries, education and local services. We prepare greeting messages, quick replies and a simple process for your team, so every chat gets a prompt, helpful answer.

Meta Ads for Ecommerce and Shopify Stores

For online stores, we send visitors to product and collection pages and track purchases. Our Shopify and WordPress development work supports this with fast, trackable pages, clear delivery details and checkout options Indian buyers expect.

How We Choose the Right Audience

We begin with location and a simple offer, then widen as data arrives. Past customers, website visitors and engaged followers can become custom audiences, and lookalike audiences help find similar people. For city-based businesses, a radius around your shop or clinic often works better than a whole state. We avoid over-narrow targeting, since it raises costs and slows learning.

Creative Testing: Hindi, Hinglish and English

Ads fail when creative is weak. We test:

  • Hooks: the first three seconds of a video or the first line of text.
  • Formats: single image, carousel and Reels-style video.
  • Languages: English, Hindi or Hinglish, based on your audience.
  • Offers: a free consultation, a discount or a limited-time slot.
  • Proof: real photos, results and reviews, shown honestly.

Meta Ads for Different Industries

  • Clinics and hospitals: appointment and awareness campaigns that follow healthcare advertising rules.
  • Real estate: project launches, site-visit offers and retargeting visitors.
  • Coaching and schools: admission drives and demo-class sign-ups.
  • Retail and ecommerce: product catalogues, offers and festival campaigns.
  • Restaurants and events: local reach, bookings and event promotion.
  • Pharma and B2B: carefully reviewed campaigns that follow ad policies and regulations.

Meta has advertising policies, and some categories carry extra rules. We review your ads against current policies and your industry's regulations before launch.

How Much Do Meta Ads Cost in India?

Your cost depends on your audience, competition, season, creative quality, offer and landing page. Festival periods and crowded industries often raise costs. We suggest a starting budget based on your goal and area, then adjust as data arrives. Your ad budget goes directly to Meta, and our management fee is separate and agreed in writing, so you always see what you spend on ads and what you pay for management.

A Simple 60-Day Meta Ads Process

  • Days 1 to 7: goal review, tracking set-up, audience plan and first creatives.
  • Days 8 to 21: launch two or three offers with several creatives each and keep budgets steady while data arrives.
  • Days 22 to 45: pause weak ads, scale the best and test new hooks.
  • Days 46 to 60: refine audiences, improve the landing page and plan the next quarter.

Lead Quality and Follow-Up

A lead is only valuable if someone responds. We help you set up quick replies, short WhatsApp message templates and a simple way to mark leads as good or poor. That feedback lets us adjust targeting and creatives toward people who actually buy.

Tracking and Reporting

We track calls, form fills, WhatsApp chats and purchases, not only clicks. The monthly report shows spend, cost per lead or purchase, lead quality feedback, creative performance and next steps in plain language.

Meta Ads, Google Ads and SEO: What to Use When

  • Meta Ads: best for creating demand, showing offers and reaching people who are not searching yet.
  • Google Ads: best for people who search for your service right now.
  • SEO: best for lasting visibility and trust. See our SEO and AI SEO services.

Many businesses use all three, and each makes the others stronger.

Common Meta Ads Mistakes We Avoid

  • Boosting posts without a goal.
  • Sending traffic to a slow or confusing page.
  • Skipping tracking.
  • Changing too many things at once.
  • Ignoring lead quality and follow-up speed.
  • Promising guaranteed results.

What We Need From You

Please share your offer, service area, best-selling products or services, photos and videos, and a person who can answer leads quickly. Access to your Facebook Page, Instagram account and website helps us set up tracking correctly.
