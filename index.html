Frequently Asked Questions

What does a digital marketing company in India do? +

It helps businesses get found and contacted online through SEO, websites, social media, video and paid ads, then tracks results such as enquiries and sales.

Which digital marketing services does a small business need first? +

Usually a fast mobile website, a Google Business Profile and local SEO. Social media and Meta Ads come next, based on your goals and budget.

What is AI SEO (AEO and GEO)? +

AEO and GEO adapt SEO for answers from Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity, using clear answers, schema and consistent brand information.

Do I need an llms.txt file to appear in Google AI Overviews? +

No. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode use the same Search index and fundamentals, with no special file required. We may add llms.txt as an optional extra.

How much does digital marketing cost in India? +

It depends on services, competition and goals. For Meta Ads, our management fee is always separate from your ad budget. We quote after a free consultation.

Should I choose WordPress or Shopify? +

WordPress suits service and content websites. Shopify suits online stores that need products, checkout and shipping.

Can you make changes to my existing website? +

Yes. We handle edits, new pages, speed fixes, redesigns, security updates and maintenance on WordPress, Shopify and custom sites.

Do you offer AI video creation for social media? +

Yes. We make Reels and short videos with AI-assisted tools plus human scripting, editing and review, in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

What do your Meta Ads services include? +

Facebook and Instagram campaign setup, audience targeting, creative testing, lead forms or WhatsApp ads, conversion tracking where supported, and regular optimisation.

What is the difference between SEO and Meta Ads? +

SEO builds organic visibility in search over time. Meta Ads give paid visibility on Facebook and Instagram quickly. Many businesses use both.

How long does SEO take to show results? +

It depends on competition, your site condition and how consistently changes are made. We never promise rankings or fixed timelines.
