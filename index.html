Frequently Asked Questions

What services does a digital marketing company in India offer?+

A digital marketing company in India typically offers SEO, website development, social media marketing, video content and paid ads such as Meta Ads, then tracks results like enquiries, calls and sales.

How do I choose the right digital marketing company in India?+

Look for clear reporting, realistic promises, relevant case studies and a plan built around your goals and budget. Be careful with anyone who guarantees rankings or a fixed number of leads.

What does an SEO company in India do?+

An SEO company improves your website's technical health, local presence, content and links so the right customers can find you on Google. Work usually includes an audit, on-page fixes, Google Business Profile support and monthly reporting.

How long does SEO take to show results?+

It depends on competition, your site's condition and how consistently changes are made. We never promise rankings or fixed timelines, but we do share Search Console data so progress is visible.

What is an AI SEO company and what does it do?+

An AI SEO company prepares your website to be understood and cited by Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity, in addition to normal search. That means clear answers, schema, crawlable pages and consistent business information.

What is AEO (answer engine optimisation) and how is it different from SEO?+

AEO focuses on giving direct, well-structured answers to real customer questions so search engines and AI assistants can quote them. It builds on SEO fundamentals rather than replacing them.

What is GEO (generative engine optimisation)?+

GEO helps AI tools describe your business correctly by keeping your details, reviews and mentions consistent across the web. No one can guarantee that an AI tool will cite you, so we focus on the fundamentals.

What do Meta Ads management services include?+

Facebook and Instagram campaign setup, audience targeting, creative testing, lead forms or WhatsApp ads, conversion tracking where supported, and regular optimisation.

Is the Meta Ads management fee included in the ad budget?+

No. Our management fee is always separate from the budget you pay to Meta. The right ad budget depends on your goals, location and competition, and we suggest it after a free consultation.

How does social media marketing help my business get leads?+

Consistent posts, Reels and short videos build trust and reach new people, and clear calls to action send them to WhatsApp, a call or your website. We measure enquiries, not just likes.
