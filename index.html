Common SEO Mistakes Businesses Should Avoid

Even SEO-conscious organizations often stand in their own way, thanks to common blunders such as:

  • Creating duplicate content or thinly written articles
  • Failing to optimize technical SEO
  • Having a website that’s slow to load
  • Offering a subpar mobile experience
  • Stuffing websites with more keywords than they actually need
  • Getting into a spam backlinking habit
  • Forgetting title tag and meta description fields
  • Ineffective internal website linking
  • Mismatching business details on online listings
  • Failing to update the content from time to time

Use regular SEO audits to detect and resolve this kinds of problems before they begin to take a hit at the ranking.
