Why SEO Matters in India in 2026

India's online audience keeps growing. Kepios counted 1.03 billion internet users in India in October 2025, an increase of 223 million in a year. The same report found about 500 million social media user identities.

Advertising spend follows that audience. Sensor Tower reports that India's digital advertising market passed $4 billion in 2025 and should exceed $5 billion in 2026. As paid costs climb, organic search gives you a cheaper long-term channel.

Language matters too. About 57 per cent of urban internet users prefer content in regional languages, according to IAMAI and Kantar. Good SEO and digital marketing services must reflect how real customers search.

How AI Overviews Changed SEO

Google now answers many queries directly on the results page. A randomised field study found that AI Overviews cut organic clicks by 38% on the queries where they appeared. Pew Research found users click a regular result 8% of the time with an AI Overview, compared with 15% without one.

Citations still pay off. Seer Interactive measured about 2.1% CTR for pages cited in an AI Overview and about 0.9% for pages left out. SparkToro notes that branded, local, and high-intent transactional searches stay less affected by zero-click behaviour. Local SEO therefore carries more weight than ever.

How to Choose the Best SEO Company in India

Use this checklist when you compare agencies:

  • Ask for a written strategy. Skip any agency that promises page-one rankings in 30 days.
  • Check technical skill. Page speed, mobile usability, and schema markup all affect results.
  • Review sample content. Good writers answer the searcher's question quickly and clearly.
  • Demand transparent reporting. Request monthly numbers on clicks, rankings, calls, and leads.
  • Confirm ethical link building. Avoid bulk link packages and private blog networks.
  • Match the agency to your size. An enterprise agency may overlook a small local business.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which is the best SEO company in India in 2026?

No single company fits every business. Large brands often choose agencies such as PageTraffic or Infidigit, while local businesses may prefer a regional partner like Grow with RP. Compare at least three agencies on process, reporting, and local expertise.

2. How many internet users does India have in 2026?

Kepios estimated 1.03 billion internet users in India in October 2025. That makes India one of the largest search audiences in the world.

3. How long does SEO take to work?

Most sites show early movement within three to six months. Competitive keywords can take a year or longer. Local searches often respond faster than national ones.

4. Do AI Overviews make SEO useless?

No. They change how you earn traffic. Pages cited inside an AI Overview earn more clicks than pages left out. Clear answers, strong structure, and trustworthy content raise your chances of being cited.

5. Is a local SEO company better than a national one?

For local businesses, usually yes. A local team understands regional search habits, languages, and competitors. This matters whether you need an SEO company in Mohali, Panchkula, or Kaithal.

6. What do SEO and digital marketing services include?

They usually cover keyword research, on-page and technical SEO, content creation, link building, Google Business Profile management, social media, and paid ads. The exact mix depends on your goals and budget.

7. How much does SEO cost in India?

Prices vary widely with your industry, competition, and scope. Ask every agency for a written proposal that lists deliverables, timelines, and reporting. Compare scope, not just price.
