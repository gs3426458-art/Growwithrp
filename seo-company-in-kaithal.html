The Changing Face of Local Search in 2026

Search behaviour has shifted dramatically. Nearly half of all Google searches today carry local intent — people are no longer just searching for products or services; they're searching for products or services near them, and they expect an answer immediately. Searches using phrases like "near me" have exploded in growth over the last couple of years, and a large share of the people making these searches take action — calling, visiting, or messaging a business — within 24 hours.

For a business in Kaithal, this means your Google Business Profile, your website's on-page SEO, your reviews, and your local citations are no longer optional extras. They are the storefront that most of your customers see first. Studies also show that local SEO tends to generate leads at a significantly lower cost than traditional outbound marketing, and a majority of small businesses now report that local SEO brings in more leads than paid ads alone.

There's another shift happening too: Google's search results are increasingly shaped by AI Overviews and AI-powered answer boxes. Many local-style search queries today trigger an AI-generated summary before the traditional list of blue links even appears. This means ranking well isn't just about keywords anymore — it's about how clearly and accurately your business information is structured for AI to understand and recommend. That's where an experienced, forward-thinking SEO company in Kaithal like Grow with RP becomes essential — because standing out in 2026 requires answering a different question than it did five years ago: not just "how do I rank," but "how do I get recommended."
