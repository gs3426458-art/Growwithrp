Ten Features Every Kaithal Business Website Needs

Use this checklist to judge any website, including your own:

  • Mobile-first layout: Most visitors use phones.
  • Fast loading: Pages should open in under three seconds.
  • Clear contact buttons: Add call, WhatsApp, and map buttons on every page.
  • Service pages: Give each service its own page.
  • Local details: Show your address, hours, and service areas.
  • Trust signals: Add reviews, photos, certificates, and client logos.
  • Secure connection: Use HTTPS to protect visitors.
  • Simple navigation: Visitors should find any page in two clicks.
  • Enquiry form: Keep it short, with three or four fields.
  • Analytics: Track visits, calls, and form fills.

A site with all ten features already beats most local competitors.

What Does Web Development in Kaithal Cost?

Prices depend on size, features, and design effort. Typical ranges in 2026 look like this:

  • Simple landing page: ₹5,000 to ₹15,000
  • Small business website (5–10 pages): ₹15,000 to ₹50,000
  • E-commerce store: ₹40,000 to ₹1,50,000
  • Custom web application: ₹1,50,000 and above

Treat these as starting guides, not fixed quotes. Always request a written scope that lists pages, features, revisions, and support. Also confirm who pays for hosting, domain renewal, and security tools.

A Realistic Six-Week Timeline

Clear schedules prevent delays. A typical small business website follows this plan:

  • Week 1: Discovery call, goals, and page list.
  • Week 2: Design mockups and your feedback.
  • Weeks 3–4: Development, content, and SEO setup.
  • Week 5: Testing on phones, tablets, and computers.
  • Week 6: Launch, tracking setup, and handover.

Larger projects take longer. Delays usually come from late content, so prepare your photos and text early.

Platform Choices: WordPress, Custom Code, or Builders

Owners often ask which platform to use. Here is a simple comparison:

  • WordPress: Flexible and SEO-friendly. It suits most business sites.
  • Custom code: Fast and tailored. It suits unique projects but costs more.
  • Drag-and-drop builders: Quick and cheap. However, they often limit speed and SEO control.
  • E-commerce platforms: Best for online stores with many products.

Ask your developer to explain the reasons behind the choice. A good team recommends the platform that fits your goals, not the one it finds easiest.

Speed, Security, and Mobile: The Three Pillars

Speed

Compress images, use clean code, and choose reliable hosting. Speed affects both rankings and sales.

Security

Use HTTPS, update plugins, and back up your site regularly. A hacked website damages trust and rankings.

Mobile Experience

Test every page on a real phone. Buttons should be easy to tap, and text should be easy to read without zooming.

  • Who will work on my website?
  • Will I own the website, domain, and content?
  • How will you handle speed and mobile design?
  • Do you include basic SEO setup?
  • What support do you offer after launch?
