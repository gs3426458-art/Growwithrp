What Our SEO Services Cover

Technical SEO

We check speed, mobile layout, indexing, site structure and errors, then fix the issues that hold pages back. Google's SEO Starter Guide lists the basics we follow.

Keyword research and search intent

We find the words customers use and group them by intent, such as comparing, buying or visiting nearby. Each page then targets one clear purpose.

On-page SEO and content

We improve titles, headings, descriptions, internal links and images, and write helpful pages in line with Google's advice on people-first content.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

We complete and manage your profile, keep details consistent, build location pages and help you earn honest reviews, using the factors Google explains in its guide to local ranking.

Link building and reporting

We earn links through useful content and genuine mentions, and we avoid schemes that break Google's rules. You receive a monthly report on traffic, calls and enquiries, with next steps.

AI SEO: AEO, GEO and AIO

More customers now ask full questions in AI tools. AI SEO adapts your site for those answers.

  • AEO (answer engine optimisation): direct answers to real customer questions, clear headings, FAQs and matching schema.
  • GEO (generative engine optimisation): consistent business details, trusted mentions and clear brand information so AI tools describe you correctly.
  • AIO (AI Overviews readiness): crawlable, fast, well-linked pages with original content.

Honest note: Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode use the same Search index and SEO fundamentals, and no special file is required, as explained in its page on AI features and your website. So we fix the fundamentals first. An optional llms.txt file and AI visibility checks are extras for other AI tools, never a guarantee.

What an SEO Audit Includes

Every project starts with an audit, so you see the real gaps before you spend more. We review:

  • crawling, indexing and site speed,

You receive a plain-language action list ranked by impact, not a long automated report.

Common SEO Problems We Fix

  • One page trying to rank for every service.
  • Slow mobile pages and heavy images.
  • Duplicate or copied content.
  • Missing or inconsistent business details across the web.
  • Pages that search engines cannot reach or index.
  • Weak titles and descriptions that earn few clicks.
  • No tracking, so nobody knows which pages bring enquiries.
