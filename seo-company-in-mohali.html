The 2026 Shift: AI Search, AEO & GEO for Local Businesses

Search behavior is evolving beyond traditional Google rankings, and tricity businesses need to plan for it now. AI Overviews increasingly appear on informational queries, and tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini are becoming genuine discovery channels — including for local business searches like "best SEO company in Mohali" or "top digital marketing agency near Chandigarh."

This is where AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) come in — structuring content so AI systems can directly extract and cite it as an answer. Importantly, GEO builds on top of strong traditional SEO, since AI search tools tend to pull citations from pages that already rank well organically. A modern SEO and digital marketing services strategy for Mohali, Chandigarh, or Panchkula businesses in 2026 has to account for both — traditional rankings and AI-overview visibility (AIO) — as one integrated approach, not two separate services.
